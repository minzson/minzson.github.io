## Teaching

**Teaching Assistant, Compiler Design** · Sungkyunkwan University · Spring 2018
Course by Prof. HwanSoo Han. Supported assignments and grading for a core systems course — the same semester I was doing GPU research in his lab, which made for a useful feedback loop between teaching the fundamentals and applying them.

**Teaching Assistant, Computational Thinking and SW Coding** · Sungkyunkwan University · Spring 2017
Led weekly hands-on lab sessions for 20–30 first-year students from non-CS majors. Teaching programming to students with zero background is a lesson in explanation itself: every abstraction I took for granted had to be rebuilt from scratch, in plain language.

## Research Programs

**WISET Engineering Research Team Program** · Korea Center for Women in Science, Engineering and Technology · Apr 2017 – Oct 2017

Selected for a competitive, government-funded research program supporting women in engineering (100 teams funded nationwide). As part of a 5-member team, I investigated the performance of **zswap** — Linux's compressed swap cache — in mobile environments, and techniques for improving response time under memory pressure. My first hands-on exposure to OS-level memory management, and an early push toward the systems questions I still care about.

## Community

**WITS (Women in Technology at Samsung)** · Samsung Electronics · Jan 2025 – Dec 2025

Selected for a company-wide program pairing engineers with senior women engineering leaders through 1:1 mentoring and executive sessions. A year of candid conversations about technical growth and long-term careers in engineering — including the decision to pursue graduate study.
